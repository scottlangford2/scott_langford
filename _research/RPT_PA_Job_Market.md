Three editions run off the same database: a monthly Candidate Pulse tracking what is open right now and how the season is developing; a candidate-facing annual reviewing hiring volume, rank and track composition, geography, and institutional type; and an institutional annual benchmarking programs against their peers. The underlying data are a continuously updated posting archive linked to program and placement records, which makes it possible to describe the market as it happens instead of after the fact.'
