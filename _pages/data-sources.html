Free, public data for class projects, applied research projects, and theses in public administration and policy. All of these can be used without paying for access. See also: For Students

Before you download anything, figure out the unit of observation you need (person, organization, city, county, state) and the years you need. Then read the source's documentation or codebook first: most mistakes in student projects come from a definition that changed between years, not from the analysis.
