Method & coverage

The crawl universe is NASPAA's own Roster of Accredited Programs (184 master's programs at 176 institutions), matched to IPEDS. From there we harvest three kinds of public source: university portals that publish syllabi under a state posting mandate (Texas, Indiana and North Carolina each require it by law), openly licensed courseware, and public pages on a program's own website. Harvesting obeys each site's robots.txt, runs one request at a time per host, and identifies itself. Documents hosted by commercial syllabus vendors, or behind a bot-detection challenge, are linked but never fetched.

Coverage therefore follows the law, not the discipline: the mandate states are over-represented because their institutions must publish, while a department elsewhere that posts nothing is invisible here no matter how large its program.

This page publishes metadata and derived facts only — course, term, level, topics, software, assessment types and citation lists — plus a link to the instructor's own posted copy. It does not host or reproduce syllabus text. Fields we could not parse are left blank rather than guessed. Sources so far: dept_crawler (1), ecu_feed (78), fose (556), nc_syllabus (26), ocw (24), ut_coursedocs (1,444), uta_dm (846). Of 1,046 indexed syllabi, 351 were readable in full, which is why reading lists and assessment types cover fewer courses than the table does. A further 60 rows were multiple sections of the same course and term and have been collapsed into one.
