Method & coverage

The crawl universe is NASPAA's own Roster of Accredited Programs (184 master's programs at 176 institutions), matched to IPEDS. From there we harvest two kinds of public source: university portals that publish syllabi under a state mandate, and public pages on a program's own website. Harvesting obeys each site's robots.txt, runs one request at a time per host, and identifies itself. Documents hosted by commercial syllabus vendors are linked but never fetched.

This page publishes metadata and derived facts only — course, term, level, topics, software, assessment types and citation lists — plus a link to the instructor's own posted copy. It does not host or reproduce syllabus text. Fields we could not parse are left blank rather than guessed. Sources so far: ocw (24), ut_coursedocs (1,444). Of 322 indexed syllabi, 25 were readable in full, which is why reading lists and assessment types cover fewer courses than the table does. A further 20 rows were multiple sections of the same course and term and have been collapsed into one.
