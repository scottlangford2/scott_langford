Typical PA / PP posting volume by calendar month (pooled 2016–2025, n=253). The current month is highlighted. Volume peaks in summer (Jun–Aug, ~16% in August alone), with a secondary winter–spring wave; May (2%) and December (2%) are the quiet months. Use it to calibrate where the current cycle sits — not as a year-over-year trend.

This dashboard is generated from the faculty_market_analysis panel, which joins live faculty-posting data (NASPAA, AJO, Chronicle, Inside Higher Ed) to PhD-supply data (IPEDS, NSF SED) and program placement records. "Open" means a posting still appearing in our most recent crawl; postings are deduplicated across sources and each listing's state is cross-checked against institution and city references. Every figure is an aggregate over programs, institutions, or the market — no per-person identifier is published. A companion monthly brief is available as a PDF download.
