Active and recent coauthors, grouped by the line of work we share. Affiliations are as of our most recent joint project.

## Public Administration and Public Finance

**[Andrew J. Grandage](https://www.wcu.edu/faculty/agrandage.aspx)** — Associate Professor, Political Science and Public Affairs, Western Carolina University. Public administration, capital budgeting, and project management. Coauthor on the federal hazard mitigation work.

**[Akheil Singla](https://spa.asu.edu/)** — Associate Professor, School of Public Affairs, Arizona State University. Public financial management, local government fiscal health, and municipal debt. Coauthor on local finance and local government finance.

**[Xintong Chen](https://gs.sjsu.edu/socialsciences/research/rsca-faculty-bio/chen-xintong.php)** — San Jose State University, previously the School of Public and International Affairs at NC State. Nonprofit financial health and organizational resilience in disasters. Coauthor on nonprofit survival after extreme weather.

**[Nandhini Rangarajan](https://www.polisci.txst.edu/)** — Professor, Department of Political Science, Texas State University. Public management, research methods, and public affairs education. Coauthor on local government and data.

**[Robert Hines](https://politicalscience.charlotte.edu/people/robert-hines/)** — Assistant Professor, Department of Political Science and Public Administration, UNC Charlotte. Public budgeting and financial management. Coauthor on the federal hazard mitigation work and lead author on the program-type cost-savings analysis.

**Hina Chaudhry** — Doctoral student, Department of Political Science and Public Administration, UNC Charlotte. Lead author on *Do Federal Hazard Mitigation Assistance Grants Pay Off?*

**[Melanie Waddell](http://www.melaniewaddell.com/)** — Doctoral candidate in Public Administration and Policy, School of Public and International Affairs, University of Georgia. Emergency management networks and the budgeting of disaster response. Coauthor on the federal hazard mitigation assistance work.

**Christina Kattikatt** — MPA student, Texas State University. Coauthor on local government and data.
