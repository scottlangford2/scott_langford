Monthly pulse check for PhD candidates — October 13, 2026

Download this edition (PDF)Live job-market dashboard

I’ve picked up 29 new PA / PP tenure-track postings since the previous snapshot (2026-09-02), including searches at University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, University of North Florida, University of California-Berkeley and 26 other institutions. Of those, 19 are Assistant Professor searches and 10 are senior or open-rank. Across all ranks, the PA and PP panels added a net 49 canonical postings (de-duplicated — one row per real search after merging cross-postings) since 2026-09-02. We’re in the fall window. October volume sits below the July–August peak; most active searches now are carry-overs nearing decision.

How we’re doing relative to expectations

I don’t have a clean year-over-year comparison for September 2026 yet — the live scrapers came online in April 2026, so prior years are Wayback-sampled and thin. The seasonality shape in the figure below is still the right calibration tool; year-over-year context sharpens as more cycles accumulate. The chart below shows the underlying shape: PA and PP postings stacked by calendar month, pooled across the complete sampled years (2016–2025). I recommend candidates use it as a calibration tool. Volume arrives in two waves — the larger in July–August and a second in January (each about 13% of the year) — while May and December are the two dead months (roughly 1% each). Read the comparison above against this typical pattern to see where the current cycle sits.
