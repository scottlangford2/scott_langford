An ongoing, open data project measuring the U.S. public administration (PA)
academic faculty job market — a series that PA, unlike economics and political
science, has never had. It joins live faculty-posting data (NASPAA, AJO,
Chronicle, Inside Higher Ed) to PhD-supply data (IPEDS completions, NSF SED) and
to program placement records, and reports the aggregate trends: how many
tenure-track searches are running, when in the year they open, where they are,
and where PhDs are placed.

The **candidate edition** distills the current data into a short monthly brief
for people on the job market.

- **Latest monthly brief:** [📄 Download PDF]({{ '/files/pa_faculty_job_market_monthly.pdf' | relative_url }})

All underlying sources are public. Every published figure is an aggregate over
programs, institutions, or cohorts — no individual-level outcomes are reported.
