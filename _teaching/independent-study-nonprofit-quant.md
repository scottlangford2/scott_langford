Graduate independent study applying quantitative program-evaluation methods to nonprofit administrative data, working from IRS 990 filings. Covers dataset construction and measurement, data auditing, program theory and counterfactuals, regression, difference-in-differences, interrupted time series and regression discontinuity, and cost-effectiveness analysis.
